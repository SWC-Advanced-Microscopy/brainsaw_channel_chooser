          Recommender settings

          Discourages long wavelengths, where there is little background signal to give anatomical context for registering sections.

          Nothing shorter is ever suggested. Below about 760 nm the laser is less stable and the embedding agar autofluoresces heavily, so the extra cross-section is not worth having.
